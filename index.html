Calendars

    Connect a calendar
    On a computer, open Google Calendar → Settings (⚙) → the calendar under Settings for my calendars → Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format. Each calendar has its own. It's only stored where Circle's server can read it.

    Contact and email sync

    Keeps names, phones, emails, birthdays, addresses, company and photos the same in Circle and Google, both ways. Conversations, notes and life events stay in Circle. Deletions are never copied: Circle asks you first.

              Import contacts

              Choose a contacts file (.vcf, also called a vCard). One file can hold all your contacts, and you can pick several files at once. You'll choose who to bring in before anything is added.

              • iPhone, all contacts in one file: in the Contacts app, tap Lists (top left), long-press All Contacts (or any list), choose Export and save to Files.
              • iPhone, one person: open them in Contacts → Share Contact → Save to Files.
              • Google Contacts: contacts.google.com → Export → vCard.
              • iCloud on a computer: icloud.com/contacts → select all → ⚙ → Export vCard.
