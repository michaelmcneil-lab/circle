Import contacts

Choose a contacts file (.vcf, also called a vCard). You'll get to pick who to bring in before anything is added.

  • iPhone, all contacts: in the Contacts app, tap Lists, long-press All Contacts (or any list), choose Export and save to Files.
  • iPhone, one person: open them in Contacts → Share Contact → Save to Files.
  • Google Contacts: contacts.google.com → Export → vCard.
  • iCloud on a computer: icloud.com/contacts → select all → ⚙ → Export vCard.
