Circle

Privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

Circle is a personal app for keeping up with the people in its owner's life. It is used only by its owner, under their own accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What Circle stores

The people its owner adds and what they record about them: contact details, photos, conversations, follow-ups, life events and notes. This is kept in the owner's private Supabase database, protected so that only the owner's signed-in account can read it.

Google Contacts

When the owner connects their Google account, Circle reads and updates that account's contacts in order to keep the two in step: names, nicknames, phone numbers, email addresses, birthdays, addresses, company, job title and contact photos.

  • Google contact data is used only to show and sync the owner's own contacts between Circle and Google Contacts.
  • It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone except as described under Other services below.
  • Circle's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • The Google sign-in is stored only on Circle's server, never on a device.

Gmail

If the owner turns on email activity, Circle reads the headers of new emails in their Gmail account: who sent them, who they went to, the date and the subject. It cannot read what an email says. It uses these only to add an "Email" entry, with the subject, to the timeline of people already in Circle.

  • Emails with anyone not in Circle, mailing lists, and emails to more than five people are ignored and not stored.
  • Gmail data is not sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone. It is not sent to any AI model, except that the owner's own Claude connector can read a person's Circle timeline, these entries included, when the owner asks it about that person.
  • Circle's use of Gmail data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Turning email activity off in Circle stops it at once; removing Circle's access at myaccount.google.com/connections ends it entirely.

Other services

  • Supabase hosts Circle's data and server functions.
  • Anthropic (Claude) sorts notes the owner chooses to send to the Inbox into suggested updates. To match a note to the right person, it is given the names, nicknames and companies of the people in Circle, some of which may have come from Google Contacts. It is not given phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, birthdays, photos or anything from Gmail. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Calendars the owner connects are read to show upcoming meetings.

Removing access and data

In Circle, ⋯ → Google Contacts sync → Disconnect Google stops syncing and forgets which contacts are linked, without deleting any contacts. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. The owner can export or delete all of Circle's data at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the support email shown on Circle's Google sign-in screen.
